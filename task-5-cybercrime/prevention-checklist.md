# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

## General Safety
- [ ] Never share your Aadhaar number, PAN, or bank details over 
      phone or email unless you initiated the contact
- [ ] Use strong, unique passwords for every account and enable 
      two-factor authentication (2FA)
- [ ] Regularly check your bank statements for any transactions 
      you did not make
- [ ] Avoid oversharing personal details (date of birth, phone 
      number, address) on social media
- [ ] Always log out of accounts when using shared or public 
      computers
- [ ] Keep your phone and laptop software updated to patch 
      security vulnerabilities

## Financial & UPI Safety
- [ ] Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone — not even 
      someone claiming to be from your bank
- [ ] Only use official UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) downloaded 
      from the official Play Store or App Store

## If You Are a Victim
- Report immediately at the **National Cyber Crime Portal**: 
  cybercrime.gov.in
- Call the **Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930** (available 24x7)
